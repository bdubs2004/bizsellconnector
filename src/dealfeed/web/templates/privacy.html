Privacy and removal requests

What's here

Deal Feed lists established businesses from public state business registries ({{ COVERED_STATES }}) and public SBA loan data. It holds business-level information only: the business's name, the address on its public filing, its industry, size estimates, and the names of registered agents or officers as they appear in public filings.

What isn't

No home addresses of people, ages, phone numbers, personal emails, or data from people-search sites. Registered-agent and officer addresses, owner demographics, and individual (sole-proprietor) loan records are dropped when data is loaded and never stored.

Remove a business

If you own or represent a business listed here and want it removed, email {{ contact }} with the business name and state. Removed businesses disappear from search, profiles, exports, saved lists and AI tools, and stay removed when data refreshes.

Your account

We store your email, plan, saved targets, notes and buy box. API keys are stored only as one-way hashes. Your saved targets, notes and buy box are visible only to you.
