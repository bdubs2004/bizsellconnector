How the score works

Age

Businesses filed 20 to 30+ years ago are more likely to have owners heading toward retirement.

Owner-run signals

An individual registered agent, rather than a filing service, suggests the founder still runs it.

Fit for SBA buyers

PPP-based size estimates show whether the business is big enough to carry SBA debt.

Already sold?

A recent SBA 7(a) loan marked "Change of Ownership" means someone just bought it, so it ranks lower.
