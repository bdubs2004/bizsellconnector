What goes into a score

Rules you can read, not a black box
01 / Age

Owners near retirement

Businesses filed 20 to 30+ years ago are more likely to have a founder thinking about the exit.

02 / Control

Still owner-run

An individual registered agent, rather than a filing service, suggests the founder still runs it.

03 / Size

Bankable with SBA debt

PPP-based size estimates show whether the business can carry an SBA 7(a) loan.

04 / Recency

Not already sold

A recent 7(a) loan marked "change of ownership" means someone just bought it, so it ranks lower.
